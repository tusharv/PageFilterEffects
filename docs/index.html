Page Filter Effects

Transform any website with instant visual filters. Adjust blur, grayscale, sepia, brightness, contrast, hue rotation, saturation, and invert — your preferences are saved per site.

Free • Lightweight • No tracking

Key Features

🎛️ Real‑time Controls

See changes instantly as you move sliders. No page reloads.

🧠 Per‑Site Memory

Your chosen filters are remembered automatically for each website.

🧩 Works Everywhere

Compatible with most sites and web apps, with minimal performance impact.

↩️ One‑click Reset

Return to the original look instantly at any time.

How to Use

  1. Open any website
  2. Click the Page Filter Effects extension icon
  3. Adjust the sliders to your preference
  4. Enjoy — your settings are saved for that site

Privacy First

100% local. Your preferences are stored on your device (Chrome storage/localStorage). We do not collect analytics, telemetry, or personal data.
